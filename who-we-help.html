Being honest about fit.

We would rather turn down a project on the first call than take money for something that was never going to work.

Good fit

You will get value from this

  • Roughly 5 to 150 people, with an operating business rather than an idea
  • Inbound inquiries matter to you, whether that is 10 a month or 300
  • One person can make the decision without a committee
  • Repetitive admin is taking time that should go to sales or delivery
  • You want to own the system afterwards, not rent it forever
  • You would rather have a working thing in five weeks than a perfect thing in a year
Poor fit

We would point you elsewhere

  • You need a large custom software platform built from scratch
  • You are pre-revenue and looking for a technical co-founder
  • You want paid media buying, PR, or content produced at volume
  • The main goal is the cheapest possible website, in which case a template will serve you better and we will say so
  • Nobody internally has time to give feedback for a few weeks
  • You need enterprise procurement, security review, and a vendor panel
