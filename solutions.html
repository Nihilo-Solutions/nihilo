Website Rebuild & Conversion System

The problem. Most small business websites were built once, by whoever was available, and have not been touched since. They load slowly on phones, bury the offer three clicks deep, and leave a visitor with nothing obvious to do. Traffic is rarely the real issue. The page is.

Book a call

  • Mobile-first design, tested on real phones rather than a desktop preview
  • A clear offer and one obvious next step on every page
  • Lead capture forms wired to your inbox and your CRM, with delivery verified

AI Lead Capture & Follow-up System

The problem. Response speed is where small businesses lose work without ever seeing it happen. Inquiries arrive after hours, on weekends, and in the middle of a job, and the person who should answer is busy doing the actual work. By the time someone replies, the prospect has usually contacted someone else. Follow-up then depends on whoever remembers.

Book a call

What is included

  • Site chat that answers common questions in your voice, using your own content
  • Instant response to form submissions and after-hours inquiries, every day of the week
  • Qualifying questions that separate a real inquiry from a tire-kicker or a sales pitch
  • Routing rules so the right person is notified on the right channel: email, text, Teams, or Slack
  • Every lead logged to your CRM or a shared sheet, with the full conversation attached
  • Follow-up sequences that keep going until someone replies or opts out
  • Clean escalation to a human at any point, with context passed along
  • Guardrails: it does not quote prices, promise dates, or invent answers unless you tell it to

Where it stops

  • It opens the door and holds it. People still close the deal
  • Not outbound prospecting, cold email, or list buying
  • If it does not know an answer, it says so and hands off rather than guessing
  • CRM licences and messaging costs are billed by those vendors, not by us
  • We do not take over your inbox or reply as you without your sign-off on the wording
Typical timeline 2 to 3 weeks

03 / Package

Operations Automation Pack

The problem. In most small businesses the same handful of tasks are done by hand every single day: copying details between two systems, chasing an unanswered quote, reminding a customer about an appointment, telling the team something arrived. None of it is difficult. All of it quietly consumes the owner's week, and every manual hand-off is somewhere a job can stall.

Book a call

What is included

  • A mapping session where we follow how work actually moves through your business today
  • Two to four workflows chosen for return, not for how impressive they sound
  • Common candidates: lead routing, quote and status follow-up, appointment reminders, internal notifications, and cutting double entry between tools
  • Built and tested against real cases before it touches a customer
  • Connected to the tools you already pay for
  • A visible record of what ran and when, so you can check it rather than trust it
  • Failure alerts, so a broken workflow does not fail silently for a month
  • Written documentation and a walkthrough so your team can run it without us

Where it stops

  • Not an ERP rollout or a full systems replacement
  • Not custom software. If that is what you need, we will tell you
  • We automate the process you have. If the process itself is broken, we will say so before automating it
  • We do not advise on headcount. This is about giving time back, and what you do with it is your call
  • Additional workflows beyond the agreed four are quoted separately or added to a retainer
Typical timeline 3 to 5 weeks
Ideal fit A team with repeatable processes running on manual effort
Scope 2 to 4 workflows, agreed up front
