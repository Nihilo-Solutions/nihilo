## How we think about the work

### Start from the process
If we cannot watch or reconstruct last week's run, we do not build. Automation without a real process is just another tool.

### Nobody has to log in
The point is that the work stops landing on someone's desk. Your team should notice the output arriving, not a new system to log into.

### Stay on the work
The people who scoped it stay on it after it goes live. That is the whole model.

Based in Connecticut. We work across the Northeast and take remote engagements nationwide.
