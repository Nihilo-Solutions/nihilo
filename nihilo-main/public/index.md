How an engagement runs

## You work with the people who will build it.
- 01

### Discovery call
Thirty minutes on the process that is costing you time. What happens today, who does it, where the data lives, what done looks like.

- 02

### Map the process
We write the workflow down: inputs, exceptions, systems, and the output your team actually needs. Then a scoped build, not a platform overhaul.

- 03

### Build the automation
We construct it against your real process. You review it on your data. We adjust until it runs the way your operation runs.

- 04

### Live
We put it live and keep it running. Your team gets the output and a written description of the rules, not another system to operate.
